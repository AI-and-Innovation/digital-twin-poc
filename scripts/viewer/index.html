What you are looking at

A simulation of Terminal 1 that produces the same records real sensors and an Esri pipeline would. Every number on this page comes from the same simulated passengers, so the queue counts, zone counts and dots always agree.

1. PassengersFlights create passengers. They walk the terminal's walkways, queue at entry, check-in, security and immigration, dwell airside, board, or collect bags and leave.
↓
2. Four feeds (Kafka topics or files)Positions every 5 s · checkpoint queues every minute · zone occupancy every minute · journey KPIs every minute. Flat JSON with longitude/latitude, the shape ArcGIS Velocity ingests.
↓
3. AnalysisThis page does what Velocity and ArcGIS Dashboards would: one floor at a time, density colouring, heatmap, alerts when a checkpoint passes its wait target, individual tracks and a replay.
↓
4. ActionOpen another lane where a queue is over target, send staff to a crowded zone, watch how long passengers take from kerb to airside.

Where the map comes from

Terminal outline, International Departures and GA buildings and the bus gates: OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Floors, zones, checkpoints and walkways inside are a schematic, not the floor plan. GHIAL drawings replace them without changing anything else.

Real sensors would be

3D people counters at checkpoints (queue length and wait), Wi-Fi/BLE or camera analytics for positions (anonymised), boarding-pass and DigiYatra scans for checkpoint events.
